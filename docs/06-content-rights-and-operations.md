# Content Rights and Operations

## Rights rule

“Free online” is not a licence.

Every item must be classified as one of:

- Jalwa-owned;
- contractually licensed;
- commercially reusable open licence;
- verified public domain;
- official embed;
- external link only;
- rejected.

## Automatically permitted licence classes

Subject to item-level review:

- CC0;
- Public Domain Mark with jurisdiction review;
- CC BY;
- CC BY-SA.

## Manual review

- CC BY-ND;
- government media terms;
- archival films;
- public-domain claims;
- identifiable people;
- trademarks;
- music and performances;
- third-party materials inside otherwise open works;
- content with jurisdiction-specific status.

## Reject for a monetised self-hosted catalogue

- CC BY-NC;
- CC BY-NC-SA;
- CC BY-NC-ND;
- all rights reserved;
- unknown licence;
- “copyright-free” without evidence;
- downloaded social video;
- television clips;
- sports footage;
- dramas and films without rights;
- music, naat or recitation recordings without rights;
- news photographs and newspaper articles without permission.

## Publishing workflow

```text
Discovered
→ Imported as draft
→ Source verified
→ Rights reviewed
→ Editorial reviewed
→ Sensitivity reviewed where required
→ Scheduled
→ Published
→ Periodically rechecked
→ Removed or renewed
```

No API import may auto-publish.

## Required rights fields

- original source URL;
- creator;
- copyright holder;
- licence and version;
- licence URL;
- commercial use;
- redistribution;
- modification;
- share-alike;
- attribution text;
- changes made;
- item-level evidence;
- jurisdiction note;
- reviewer;
- verification date;
- expiry/recheck date.

## Source ingestion modes

### Allowlisted YouTube channel

- fetch metadata through API;
- verify embeddable state;
- create draft;
- editor rewrites local title and description;
- preserve source title and channel;
- publish through official embed.

### Wikimedia Commons

- search API;
- open original file page;
- inspect licence and creator;
- import metadata;
- save evidence;
- download approved asset;
- generate attribution;
- use inside an original article or story.

### Openverse

Use only as a discovery layer. Verify the original source page before importing.

### Tanzil

Import verified Quran text and structural metadata. Keep text unchanged and display required source notice. Treat translations, tafsir and recitations separately.

### NASA

Use factual educational material after checking item credits. Acknowledge NASA, avoid implying endorsement and exclude third-party-marked content unless separately cleared.

### Pexels/Pixabay

Use as production ingredients for Jalwa-created programmes. Do not republish an unmodified stock catalogue.

### Blender open movies

Check each film's licence page. Retain credits and attribution. Urdu subtitles or dubbing count as adaptations and must comply with the applicable licence.

### CGIAR and agricultural research

Use item-level materials carrying a commercially compatible open licence. CGIAR encourages open access but individual outputs may vary. Turn verified source material into locally reviewed Urdu explainers rather than dumping publications into the portal.

### FAO

FAO's default publication terms generally permit private study, research, teaching and non-commercial products. Commercial Jalwa use needs item-specific open terms or permission. Prefer official embeds, citations and permission-cleared adaptations.

## Content operations roles

- head of content;
- category editor;
- rights reviewer;
- Urdu/Roman Urdu editor;
- video editor;
- subject reviewer for Deen;
- subject reviewer for agriculture;
- child-safety reviewer;
- support/takedown owner.

One person may cover multiple roles at MVP, but the approval actions should remain distinct in the system.

## Takedown workflow

1. Receive complaint.
2. Immediately identify content and hosting mode.
3. Restrict or unpublish when credible risk exists.
4. Preserve evidence and audit history.
5. Contact source or rights holder.
6. Decide restore, amend or permanently remove.
7. Record outcome.
8. Recheck related items from the same source.

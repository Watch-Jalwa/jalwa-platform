# Launch Runbook

## Thirty days before launch

- merchant onboarding submitted;
- domain and DNS confirmed;
- production accounts created;
- privacy and subscription terms drafted;
- content source allowlist approved;
- first 100 content items in review;
- support mailbox and escalation owner created;
- analytics event dictionary frozen;
- production backup policy tested.

## Fourteen days before launch

- production checkout sandbox passes;
- webhook replay tested;
- entitlement expiry tested;
- refund support flow rehearsed;
- mobile performance tested;
- Urdu layout reviewed;
- rights evidence completeness report above target;
- Ask Jalwa citation tests pass;
- staff roles and MFA confirmed;
- incident contacts recorded.

## Seven days before launch

- content freeze for launch hero and categories;
- price and offer approved;
- production payment credentials loaded;
- live merchant transaction conducted;
- reconciliation compared to provider;
- error alerts active;
- status page or incident banner capability ready;
- rollback build identified;
- customer support macros ready.

## Launch day

### Technical

- verify DNS;
- verify TLS;
- smoke-test home, search, player, login and checkout;
- verify analytics;
- verify AI quotas;
- verify media token expiry;
- watch logs and payment events;
- preserve deployment version.

### Content

- confirm featured items available;
- verify all external embeds;
- inspect licence display;
- monitor complaints;
- schedule first seven days of updates.

### Commercial

- test monthly and annual purchase;
- confirm receipt;
- confirm settlement visibility;
- track checkout abandonment;
- publish refund and cancellation route.

## First week operating rhythm

Daily:

- new users;
- active viewers;
- payment attempts and success;
- premium activations;
- content availability;
- playback errors;
- AI cost;
- complaints;
- support backlog.

Weekly:

- retained viewers;
- conversion;
- churn/renewal;
- top categories;
- underperforming rows;
- content supply;
- gross margin;
- planned experiments.

## Rollback triggers

- duplicate charging;
- entitlement granted without verified payment;
- broad playback outage;
- exposed private media;
- serious rights complaint;
- AI leaking private or unpublished material;
- authentication bypass.

## Manual fallback

If payment callback is delayed:

- keep order pending;
- do not activate from screenshot alone;
- support verifies provider portal;
- finance records a manual reconciliation;
- authorised staff may grant a time-limited entitlement with reason and audit entry.

## Post-launch experiments

- monthly price;
- annual discount;
- free AI allowance;
- home category order;
- signup timing;
- premium preview length;
- low-data default;
- Urdu vs Roman Urdu metadata;
- Kissan seasonal landing pages;
- creator-led programmes.

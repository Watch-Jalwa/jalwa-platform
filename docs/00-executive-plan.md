# Executive Product Plan

## Product thesis

Jalwa can win by becoming the easiest place for Pakistani users to discover useful, safe and locally relevant video and knowledge content in Urdu, Roman Urdu and English.

The product should not launch as “everything for everyone.” It should launch with four high-intent content pillars:

1. **Deen and family-safe content**
2. **Kissan, farming and rural livelihood**
3. **Skills, technology, education and rozgar**
4. **Positive entertainment, shorts and Pakistan discovery**

These pillars can share one streaming and catalogue platform while addressing distinct user needs.

## Strategic differentiation

Jalwa should add value that source platforms do not provide:

- Pakistani curation;
- Urdu and Roman Urdu summaries;
- trusted categories and playlists;
- low-data playback;
- source and licence transparency;
- guided learning paths;
- “Ask Jalwa” grounded answers;
- local payment options;
- creator and institutional partnerships;
- an editorially safe children and family mode.

A plain aggregation of YouTube videos would be weak and may violate YouTube's requirement that API clients provide sufficient independent value.

## MVP commercial promise

### Free

- browse all categories;
- watch official embeds;
- selected Jalwa-hosted content;
- advertisements and sponsorship placements;
- one profile;
- basic search;
- bookmarks and history;
- a small daily AI allowance;
- standard playback quality for Jalwa-hosted media.

### Premium

- ad-free Jalwa interface;
- full authorised Jalwa catalogue;
- early or exclusive Jalwa Originals;
- enhanced playback quality where available;
- larger AI allowance;
- structured learning paths and quizzes;
- premium playlists;
- priority access to creator programmes;
- family profiles after launch;
- offline access only for content Jalwa is authorised to make downloadable.

Premium must not promise removal of advertisements inside an embedded YouTube player.

## Pricing hypothesis

The attached product deck proposes PKR 299 monthly and PKR 4,999 yearly. That annual price is higher than twelve monthly payments:

- 12 × 299 = PKR 3,588
- proposed annual = PKR 4,999

Recommended initial test:

- **Monthly:** PKR 299
- **Annual:** PKR 2,999
- **Founding offer:** optional PKR 199 for the first paid month

Treat these as hypotheses and test conversion, payment success, renewal and retention before locking them.

## North-star metric

**Weekly engaged viewers who complete a meaningful content action**, where meaningful means one of:

- watch at least five minutes;
- complete a short;
- save a content item;
- finish a quiz;
- ask a grounded AI question;
- return to continue watching.

## Primary business metrics

- visitor-to-account conversion;
- free-to-paid conversion;
- checkout completion;
- monthly and annual renewal;
- paid churn;
- weekly retained viewers;
- watch minutes by hosting mode;
- gross margin per subscriber;
- AI cost per active user;
- content acquisition cost;
- complaint and takedown rate;
- licensed-content availability rate.

## Current product scope exclusions

Jalwa's approved delivery model is a mobile-first responsive web application and installable PWA. Native iOS/Android applications and Google Play/Apple App Store distribution are not planned in the current product roadmap.

- native iOS or Android applications and app-store distribution;
- DRM;
- user uploads;
- comments;
- social following;
- live chat;
- original live television infrastructure;
- licensed cricket or drama catalogues;
- family profiles;
- offline downloads;
- complex recommendation ML;
- multiple microservices;
- a separate search cluster;
- automatic publishing from third-party APIs.

## Launch definition

Jalwa is ready for public paid launch when:

- the domain and production environment are live;
- at least 150 approved catalogue items exist;
- at least 30 items are Jalwa-owned or self-hostable;
- at least 25 are localised into Urdu or Roman Urdu;
- checkout, payment callback, entitlement and expiry work end to end;
- refunds and manual entitlement correction are operational;
- AI answers cite catalogue sources;
- content rights records are complete;
- terms, privacy, refund and takedown pages are published;
- critical playback, security and payment tests pass;
- analytics can explain acquisition, engagement and conversion.

## Homepage structure

1. **Hero**
   - one editorial feature;
   - no autoplay video;
   - free/premium badge;
   - language and duration;
   - primary play action.

2. **Continue Watching**
   - authenticated users only.

3. **Jalwa Shorts**
   - vertically formatted content;
   - original or verified self-hostable only.

4. **Popular in Pakistan**
   - based on Jalwa engagement, not fabricated popularity.

5. **Deen**
   - Quran, duas, Seerah, scholar-reviewed programmes and official embeds.

6. **Kissan & Farming**
   - seasonal crop guidance, livestock, irrigation, market literacy and verified explainers.

7. **Learn, Tech & Rozgar**
   - digital skills, AI, freelancing, careers and education.

8. **Entertainment**
   - open films, creator partnerships, family-safe programmes and Jalwa Originals.

9. **Pakistan**
   - travel, heritage, culture, food and history.

10. **Kids & Family**
    - child-safe, manually approved catalogue.

11. **Live Now**
    - official embeds only at MVP.

12. **Upgrade**
    - benefits based on Jalwa-owned service value.

## Content detail page

- title and metadata;
- player or reader;
- premium status;
- source and attribution;
- Urdu and English description;
- save/share;
- Ask Jalwa entry;
- transcript or article body;
- quiz where available;
- related collection;
- complaint and rights-report action.

## Shorts experience

- one active video at a time;
- swipe or scroll vertically;
- mute state persists;
- captions on by default when available;
- prefetch only the next item;
- no infinite unbounded preloading;
- content source visible;
- save, share and “learn more”;
- premium badge only on Jalwa-controlled media.

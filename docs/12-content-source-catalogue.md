# Content Source Catalogue

This catalogue is a discovery starting point, not blanket clearance. Public access, search-engine visibility or an aggregator result never proves commercial reuse rights. Create a structured content-source issue and verify every programme and item before publication.

See [Content, commerce and deployment handoff](17-content-commerce-and-deployment-handoff.md) for the first staging pilot.

## Delivery modes

| Mode | Default rule |
|---|---|
| Official embed | Use the provider's official player and terms. Do not download or mirror the media. Do not place it behind Premium by default. |
| External link | Link to the canonical publisher when embedding or reuse is not authorized. |
| Rights-cleared self-hosting | Require item-level evidence covering territory, duration, commercial use, editing/subtitles, attribution and takedown. |
| Text/database integration | Preserve source/version notices and verify redistribution terms. Sacred or authoritative text must not be silently altered. |
| Jalwa original/adaptation | Clear source material, contributors, music, performers, locations, thumbnails and promotional use. |

## Approval tiers

### Tier A — programme approved for draft imports

A source may create drafts only when:

- official identity and canonical domain/channel are verified;
- delivery mode is fixed;
- rights position is clear for that mode;
- editorial/safety risk is understood;
- availability and takedown contacts exist;
- a review owner and next review date are recorded.

Tier A never authorizes automatic publication.

### Tier B — item approved for publication

Each item additionally requires:

- canonical source URL and publisher/creator;
- licence/contract/permission reference;
- retained evidence and evidence date;
- exact attribution text;
- territory, duration and expiry;
- commercial/Premium compatibility;
- editing, subtitle and thumbnail permissions;
- editorial/age/specialist review where relevant;
- takedown owner and immediate unpublish path.

### Tier C — Jalwa-owned master

Jalwa originals require:

- contributor agreements;
- music, performer and location releases;
- source-material and stock-asset evidence;
- final master ownership/licence record;
- delivery specifications and archive location;
- promotional, subtitle, edit and monetisation rights.

## Initial source programmes

### Jalwa Kissan

Create original 45–90 second Urdu/Roman Urdu explainers using:

- dated, cited agricultural facts;
- local agronomist or qualified reviewer;
- openly licensed/contracted visuals;
- crop, province and season labels;
- practical limitations and safety notes;
- source card and review date.

Pilot topics:

- wheat calendar;
- cotton pest awareness;
- water-saving practices;
- livestock hygiene;
- soil testing basics;
- farm record keeping;
- weather and crop-risk literacy;
- market-price literacy;
- solar pumping and groundwater awareness.

### Jalwa Deen

- Quran reader with preserved source/version notice;
- daily Ayah cards;
- original dua cards;
- one-minute Seerah explainers;
- scholar-reviewed educational content;
- official lecture embeds;
- Ramadan and Eid collections.

Record the reviewing scholar/committee and avoid presenting generated text as authoritative religious guidance.

- English for work;

- open animation with complete credits;

- approved creator channels through official embeds.

## Creator licence checklist

A creator/institution agreement should address:

- ownership and authority to license;
- licence grant, platforms and delivery mode;
- territory and duration;
- exclusivity/non-exclusivity;
- editing, clipping, dubbing, subtitles and thumbnails;
- advertising, sponsorship and subscription monetisation;
- promotional use;
- music, performer, location and third-party clearances;
- content standards and factual responsibility;
- delivery specifications and replacement files;
- takedown, expiry and termination;
- payment/revenue share, tax and statements;
- warranties, indemnity and dispute process;
- archive and post-termination handling.

## Source approval score

Score each source on:

- rights clarity;
- Pakistani relevance;
- editorial quality and safety;
- update frequency and availability reliability;
- localisation effort;
- brand and misinformation risk;
- takedown responsiveness;
- technical delivery quality;
- commercial/Premium compatibility.

High scores can reduce repetitive programme review, but they never remove item-level publication evidence.

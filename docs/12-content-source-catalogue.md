# Content Source Catalogue

This catalogue is a starting point, not blanket clearance. Verify every item.

| Source | Main use | Delivery | Commercial position | Editorial action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube approved channels | lectures, tutorials, live, podcasts | official embed | embed under platform terms | add local metadata and independent value |
| Tanzil | Quran text | text database | verbatim use with notice and source | never modify Quran text |
| Wikimedia Commons | Pakistan, history, culture, science media | self-host approved files | licence varies per item | save evidence and attribution |
| Openverse | discover images/audio | import after original verification | index only | verify original source |
| Blender Open Movies | open entertainment | self-host or official source | check each film licence | retain credits; review derivative terms |
| NASA | science and space | self-host selected media | factual educational use under guidelines | check third-party credits and acknowledge NASA |
| Pexels | production footage | inside Jalwa originals | free-use licence with restrictions | add script, narration and editing |
| Pixabay | production assets | inside Jalwa originals | licence with standalone restrictions | do not republish raw catalogue |
| CGIAR repositories | farming research and visuals | article/adaptation or approved asset | item-level licence; many open outputs | use commercially compatible item licences only |
| FAO | farming references | citation, official embed, permission-cleared reuse | default terms are often non-commercial | request permission or use specifically open items |
| Project Gutenberg | literature | reader/adaptation after review | US public-domain focus | verify Pakistan status |
| LibriVox | audiobooks | self-host after review | US public-domain claim | verify underlying book and recording status |
| Library of Congress | historical media | self-host selected items | rights statement varies | use Free to Use/Reuse items with evidence |
| Europeana | history and culture | approved import | rights statement varies | filter to PD/CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA |
| Original creators | local entertainment and knowledge | self-host | contract | define territory, duration, exclusivity and monetisation |
| Pakistani institutions | local education/farming/public interest | embed or agreement | do not assume government content is free | use official embeds or written permission |

## Initial source programmes

### Jalwa Kissan

Create original 45–90 second videos using:

- verified agricultural facts;
- local agronomist review;
- openly licensed visuals;
- simple Urdu;
- crop and province labels;
- date of advice;
- source card.

Programme ideas:

- wheat calendar;
- cotton pest awareness;
- water-saving practices;
- livestock hygiene;
- soil testing basics;
- farm record keeping;
- weather and crop-risk literacy;
- market-price literacy;
- solar pumping and groundwater awareness.

### Jalwa Deen

- Quran reader;
- daily Ayah;
- original dua cards;
- one-minute Seerah;
- scholar-reviewed explainers;
- official lecture embeds;
- Ramadan and Eid collections.

### Jalwa Rozgar

- CV clinic;
- freelancing basics;
- scam awareness;
- AI for work;
- interview practice;
- English for work;
- small-business bookkeeping;
- digital payments literacy.

### Jalwa Entertainment

- open animation;
- commissioned comedy shorts;
- creator stories;
- interviews;
- public-domain stories adapted into Urdu;
- family-safe creator channels through embeds.

## Creator licence checklist

Contract should address:

- ownership;
- licence grant;
- territory;
- duration;
- exclusivity;
- platforms;
- editing and subtitles;
- advertising;
- subscription monetisation;
- thumbnails and promotion;
- music clearance;
- performer releases;
- takedown;
- payment/revenue share;
- warranties and indemnity;
- delivery specifications.

## Source approval score

Score each source on:

- rights clarity;
- Pakistani relevance;
- quality;
- update frequency;
- safety;
- localisation effort;
- brand risk;
- availability reliability.

Only allow automatic draft imports from high-trust sources. Never allow automatic publication.

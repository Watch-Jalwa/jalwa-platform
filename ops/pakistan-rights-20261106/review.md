## Review requirements

- Named independent rights reviewer.
- Exact master identity, duration and full closing-credit review.
- Creator or licensor authority and commissioning terms.
- Third-party material rights with item-level exceptions resolved.
- Commercial availability and territorial scope for Pakistan and intended overseas territories.
- Attribution, licence-link and adaptation requirements.
- Takedown contact.
- Expiry or recheck date.
- Dated item-level reviewer decision.

The first six candidates need a documented link from the creator or commissioning party to the exact licensed edition, together with the incorporated-material review. The creator portfolio supplies production context. [Beena Sarwar documentary portfolio](https://beenasarwar.com/documentaries/)

The two VOA candidates need programme-specific verification of government-authored material and any separately owned contributions, plus a documented territorial basis for the intended distribution. [VOA usage policy](https://www.voanews.com/p/5338.html)

Draft attribution, licence links and any adaptation notices must be checked against the applicable source terms before an item receives a dated approval. [CC BY 3.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
